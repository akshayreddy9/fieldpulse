1. Summary

MSR Pulse is one system that runs a whole survey from start to finish: building the questionnaire, collecting interviews in the field, checking quality while fieldwork is running, adding up the results, and producing the client report. It replaces the separate tools and manual Excel work MSR uses today.

2. How MSR Pulse works

Every survey follows the same five steps. Everything is connected: an interview done on a surveyor's phone appears on the live map, in the quality checks, in the results and in the client report within seconds of reaching the server.

STEP 1
Set upQuestionnaire in Telugu and English, booth list, booth targets, team.
STEP 2
InterviewPhone app: consent, audio recording, GPS, photo. Works without internet.
STEP 3
Check liveLive map, booth progress and fake-interview alerts while fieldwork runs.
STEP 4
ResultsTotals from booth up to state, comparisons, Excel export.
STEP 5
ReportAutomatic first draft, MSR adds its advice, PDF and client portal.

The problems it solves (from MSR's requirements)

TodayWith MSR Pulse
Questionnaires are built in GoSurvey, a separate tool.The questionnaire builder is part of MSR Pulse: Telugu and English, all question types, skip logic, phone preview, versions.
Booth targets and fake interviews can only be checked after the data comes back, so extra back-check rounds are needed.Quotas are tracked live per booth. Nine automatic checks score every interview the moment it arrives; supervisors review flagged interviews the same day and back-check only those.

Every client report is written from scratch.The system writes a first draft from the data (every number calculated, nothing invented). MSR adds its own advice, approves, and the client sees it in their portal or as a PDF.

3. Who uses it

Everyone signs in with their mobile number and a 6-digit SMS code. There are no passwords to remember. Each person only sees the screens their role needs, and an admin can adjust this on the "Who can see what" screen.

AnalystMSR analystsResults, booth-to-state totals, comparisons, "Ask the data", the client report, the questionnaire builder.
SurveyorField staff (any number)Phone app only: start an interview, read consent, ask questions, record, capture GPS and photo, send. Sees only their own work and the surveys they are assigned to.

4. Pulse Admin: setting up a survey

4.1 Surveys

MSR can run many surveys for many clients at the same time. Each survey has its own questionnaire, targets, team, interviews, quality reviews and report. A survey moves from Draft (being prepared) to Live (fieldwork running) to Closed. Surveyors only see live surveys they are assigned to; clients only see their own surveys.

4.2 Questionnaire builder (replaces GoSurvey)

  • Telugu and English together for every question, answer and page title. Publishing is blocked until every Telugu text is filled in.
  • All question types from the requirements: single choice and multiple choice (with "Other, please specify"), grids/rating tables, cascading lists (constituency → mandal → booth), text, number, date, GPS location and photo.
  • Pages and skip logic written in plain words ("Ask this question only if the answer to … is …").
  • Live phone preview: exactly what surveyors will see.
  • Draft, save and publish: changes are a draft until "Publish to field"; phones receive the new version within seconds. Every interview keeps the version it was collected on.
  • "Used for" setting: tells the system which question is Gender, Age, Caste, Respondent name or mobile (kept private), and the Main result. Booth quotas, privacy, totals and the report all work from this, so any questionnaire works, not only the sample one.

4.3 Booth list

MSR's list of constituencies, mandals and polling booths, with each booth's location, is uploaded from Excel using a template. The upload is checked before anything is saved: missing values, repeated booths or locations outside India are listed with their Excel row number. The booth list feeds the location question, booth targets, surveyor assignments, the live map, the "away from booth" check and all totals.

4.4 Booth targets (quotas)

Each booth has a target number of interviews, split by gender, age group and caste category (the groups come from the questionnaire). Progress fills in live as interviews arrive. Colours show met (green), behind (amber/red) and over quota (purple with "+n"). Targets can be edited for one booth or a whole mandal, or exported and imported with Excel.

4.5 Team & people, and survey teams

  • Upload surveyors and supervisors from Excel (with checks), or add them one by one. Each surveyor gets a login with their mobile number automatically.
  • Add analysts, admins and client logins from the same screen. Logins can be renamed or switched off.
  • Team for each survey: choose "Everyone" or "Only selected people", and optionally give someone different booths for one survey. A surveyor can work on several surveys at the same time.
  • See each surveyor's interviews, average length and how many were flagged. No limit on the number of surveyors or phones.

5. Pulse Field app: interviews on the phone

Surveyors use the MSR Pulse Android app (on iPhone, the same app runs from the website and can be added to the home screen). It is built for field conditions:

  • Works without internet. Every interview, recording and photo is saved on the phone first and sent automatically when signal returns. It is removed from the phone only after the server confirms it has arrived, so closing the app or losing signal never loses an interview.
  • Consent first. The consent statement is shown in Telugu and English to read aloud; recording starts before any question, and the interview cannot start without it.
  • Audio recording of every interview, in speech quality (about 1.5 MB for an 8-minute interview), to save phone storage and mobile data.
  • GPS location and time on every interview, from the phone's real GPS; a photo of the house or landmark (no faces).
  • Clear checks: required answers, number ranges, 10-digit mobile numbers; the error appears at the top and scrolls to the question.
  • The surveyor's booth and what is still needed ("Female: 3 more, Age 18-25: 6 more").
  • Several surveys: the home screen lists every survey the surveyor is on; tap to switch, also offline.
  • Always up to date: new questionnaire versions arrive automatically; if a newer app is available, the app says so with a Download button.

6. Pulse Live: checking quality during fieldwork

6.1 Live map and booth progress

Every interview appears on the map within seconds, coloured by its quality status, with booth rings showing progress against target. Filter by constituency, today or all days.

6.2 Fake-interview checks and alerts

Each interview is scored from 0 to 100 by nine automatic checks the moment it arrives. A score of 40 or more puts it in "Alerts to review", with the exact reasons:

CheckWhat it catches
Too shortInterview took under 45% of the typical length for this questionnaire.
Away from boothGPS location more than 1.5 km from the assigned booth.
Duplicate answersSame answers as another interview by the same surveyor.
Consent line not readConsent statement not detected (from the recording checker add-on).
Back-to-backStarted less than 4 minutes after the surveyor's previous interview ended.
Same spotThree or more interviews by one surveyor from the same place (within 20 m).
Straight-liningEvery row of a rating grid given the same answer.
Odd hoursStarted before 7 AM or after 9 PM.
Weak GPSGPS accuracy worse than 100 m.

Supervisors accept, send for back-check or reject each flagged interview, with a note. Rejected interviews are removed from all results automatically. "Surveyors with repeated flags" shows patterns; one click shows only that surveyor's interviews with a "back-check all" button.

6.3 Listening to the recording and seeing the photo

When reviewing an interview, the supervisor sees the surveyor, time, length, distance from the booth on a map, every answer, and can play the real recording and view the photo uploaded from the phone. Each time a recording or photo is opened, it is written in the privacy access log. Recordings and photos are kept for a period MSR chooses (section 10) and then deleted automatically.

6.4 Evening summary

Each evening's flagged interviews by constituency, with an Excel back-check list for supervisors, so back-checks focus only on suspicious interviews instead of random re-visits. Automatic sending by SMS, WhatsApp or email is not connected yet (section 13); the list is downloaded and shared today.

7. Pulse Insights: results and the client report

7.1 Results for every question

A chart and table for every question, with filters (constituency, mandal, gender, age, caste) and Excel export (summary and raw data; names and mobiles appear only as codes). Rejected interviews are excluded automatically.

7.2 Totals from booth up to state

Booth results add up automatically to mandal, constituency and state: no more merging Excel files by hand. Choose which question to total; export all levels to Excel.

7.3 Compare and trends

Compare any question with any other (for example vote intention by gender or age group), and this round with the previous round, with PDF export.

7.4 Ask the data in plain English

Type a question such as "Vote share among women by age group" and get a chart. Every answer shows how the question was understood, so it can be checked.

7.5 Client report

The system writes a first draft from the live data: executive summary, method and sample, main result, concerns, ratings and constituency snapshots. Every number is calculated from the interviews; nothing is invented. Analysts edit any paragraph; the "MSR strategic advice" section is written only by MSR, and the report cannot be approved without it. Then: MSR review → Approved → Sent to client (portal and branded PDF). An AI writing service (Claude) can polish the wording using totals only, never personal data; it will be switched on with MSR's agreement.

8. Client portal

MSR's clients sign in with their own mobile number and see only their own surveys: fieldwork progress while the survey runs, key results, and the report once MSR has approved and sent it (with PDF download). Respondents' personal details and recordings are never visible to clients.

10. Security, privacy and data protection (DPDP)

The system is designed for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. The "Security, privacy & DPDP" screen shows each item honestly as In place or Before go-live.

ProtectionStatusHow
Consent recorded for every respondentIn placeConsent read aloud and recorded before any question; the server refuses interviews without consent; refusals end the interview.
Data minimisation and privacy lockIn placeOnly the personal details marked in the questionnaire are collected; they are hidden by codes; every reveal needs a reason and is logged.
Who can see whatIn placeRole-based access checked on the server (not only on screen). Clients and surveyors can never open recordings or other people's data.
Encryption on the serverIn placeEncrypted disk and nightly encrypted backups with an encryption key owned by MSR (AWS KMS); HTTPS for all traffic.
Scheduled deletion of personal detailsIn placeNames and mobile numbers erased automatically after the retention period the admin chooses (90, 180 or 365 days); answers are kept anonymously for analysis.
Recordings and photos storage periodIn placeAdmin chooses 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days; deleted automatically afterwards, except interviews still under review, which keep their evidence until a reviewer decides. Storage used and free space are shown.
Audit trailIn placeEvery important action (publishing, approvals, logins added, data deleted, recordings opened) is recorded with who and when.
Encryption on phonesBefore go-liveInterviews waiting on the phone will be encrypted on the device itself.
Data kept in India, in MSR's own accountBefore go-liveThe test site runs in a temporary AWS account with sample data only. Real data will be kept only in MSR's own AWS account in Mumbai.
Independent security auditBefore go-liveBy a CERT-In empanelled auditor before launch, then every year.

Ownership

MSR owns everything: its data, questionnaires, reports and the system itself. The full source code is kept with complete version history and will be handed over to MSR. MSR's data, questionnaires, reports and name are never reused or shown without permission.

Testing

  • 41 automated checks run before every release (a release is refused if any fails): sign-in, who can see what, surveys and clients kept apart, survey teams, booth lists, privacy masking and reveal, recordings and photos access and deletion, report accuracy.

  • The real Android app tested on an Android 15 device emulator: first-time setup, live survey updates, update notice, maps and menus.
  • A 68-item manual checklist for testers, and a test week with testers in different places.
